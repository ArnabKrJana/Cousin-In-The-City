# Mumbai Relocation & Culture Guide

## Local Transport Rules
- **Mumbai Local Train**: Never stand near the door if you are not getting off at the next station. People will push you out. Fast trains skip smaller stations. If you are going to a small station, ensure you board a Slow train. During peak hours (8 AM - 11 AM and 6 PM - 9 PM), the trains are extremely crowded. First Class tickets are more expensive but offer slightly more breathing room.
- **Auto Rickshaws**: Unlike Delhi or Bangalore, auto drivers in Mumbai generally go strictly by the meter. You do not need to haggle. However, autos are not allowed in South Mumbai (beyond Bandra/Sion).

## Renting & Brokerage
- **Brokers**: Standard broker fee in Mumbai is exactly one month's rent. Sometimes you can negotiate it down to 15 days for a quick deal, but expect to pay 1 month.
- **Security Deposit**: Usually, landlords ask for 3 to 6 months of rent upfront as a deposit. 10-11 months is rare now but can still happen in premium areas.
- **Leave & License Agreement**: It is mandatory to register your rental agreement with the police and pay stamp duty. The cost is usually split 50/50 between landlord and tenant.

## Neighborhood Vibes
- **Bandra**: The cultural hub, full of cafes, expats, and Bollywood stars. Extremely expensive.
- **Andheri**: The corporate and media hub. Great connectivity via Metro and Train.
- **Navi Mumbai / Thane**: Planned, spacious, and much cheaper, but requires a long commute if your office is in the main city.

## Weather
- Mumbai has basically two seasons: Hot & Humid, and the Monsoons.
- From June to September, carry an umbrella and wear water-resistant shoes. Flooding is common in low-lying areas like Milan Subway and Hindmata.
